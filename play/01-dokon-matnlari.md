## English (qo'shimcha til, ixtiyoriy)
**App name (29/30):** DublajUz: Uzbek video dubbing

**Short description (62/80):** Translate video speech to Uzbek and dub it with an Uzbek voice

**Full description (1538/4000):**

DublajUz turns the speech in your video into text, translates it into Uzbek and dubs it with an Uzbek voice, producing a ready MP4.

FEATURES
• On-device speech recognition (Whisper): your video is not uploaded.
• AI translation with your own API key (Google Gemini and other providers).
• Uzbek voice: on-device ONNX voice, Gemini voice or your own recorded voice.
• Review and edit the translation before dubbing, with copy / paste buttons next to the original and translated text.
• Choose the voice method per line (Auto, ONNX, Gemini, own voice) and re-dub only the lines you changed.
• Montage: move voice blocks, re-dub a single voice and re-export the video.
• History: reopen and edit previously dubbed videos at any time.
• Subtitles (SRT) and audio (M4A) are saved separately.
• Queue several videos, convert text to speech.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a video.
2. Speech is transcribed and translated.
3. Review and approve the translation.
4. The app generates the voice, mixes it into the video and saves the MP4.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Translation needs your own AI API key (for example a free key from Google AI Studio). Voice models are downloaded on first run.
• Translation and voice are generated by AI and may contain mistakes: review results before sharing.
• Only dub videos you own or have permission to use. Do not imitate another person's voice without consent.
• The free version shows ads and has a daily limit. Pro (one-time purchase) removes ads and limits.

No account required. Your videos are processed on your device.
